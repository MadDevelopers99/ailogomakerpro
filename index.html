Professional Branding Without the Price Tag

Hiring a freelance designer for a custom logo can cost anywhere from a few hundred to several thousand dollars, and often takes days or weeks of back-and-forth revisions. Our logo maker removes that barrier entirely. Browse thousands of professionally designed marks, pick the one that fits your brand's personality, and make it yours in minutes — all without spending a rupee or a dollar.

Every template in our library is organized by industry and style, so you're never scrolling through irrelevant designs. From minimalist letter marks to detailed mascots, abstract geometric symbols to industry-specific icons, there's a starting point for almost any business idea you can imagine.

A Logo That Grows With Your Brand

A good logo isn't just a picture — it's the visual anchor of everything your business communicates, from your website and social media to business cards, packaging and signage. That's why our editor lets you fine-tune every detail: swap colors to match your brand palette, choose from multiple font families, add decorative icons, curve your text into an arch, and reposition every element exactly where you want it on the canvas.

Once you're happy with the result, download it in the resolution and format you need — whether that's a small transparent PNG for a website header or a large, high-resolution file ready for print. Your brand kit keeps your chosen colors and fonts on hand so every future design stays consistent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Is this logo maker really free to use?
Yes. Browsing templates, using the full editor, and downloading PNG or JPG files is completely free. A few advanced export formats are marked as premium and coming soon.
Do I need design experience to create a logo?
No. Pick a template close to what you want, then use simple point-and-click tools to change the text, colors, icons and layout — no design background required.
Can I move and resize elements in my logo?
Yes. Select any text, icon or shape in the editor and drag it directly on the canvas to reposition it exactly where you want it.
What file formats can I download?
PNG and JPG are supported at multiple resolutions, including transparent backgrounds. SVG export is available for templates that are vector-based.
Will my logo be unique to my business?
Every template can be customized with your own brand name, colors, fonts, icons and layout, so the finished logo reflects your specific brand rather than looking off-the-shelf.
