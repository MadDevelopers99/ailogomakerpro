Help & Support

Quick answers about how this logo maker works

How do I create a logo?

Go to Templates, pick any design close to what you want, then customize the text, colors, icons and layout in the editor.

Where are my saved logos stored?

Everything (saved logos, uploads, brand kit, favorites) is stored locally in this browser — there's no account or server. Clearing your browser data will remove them.

Can I move the logo or text around?

Yes — in the editor, click "Select" next to a text block (or on the image/icon/shape panels), then drag directly on the canvas to reposition it.

Can I curve my text?

In the Text panel, use the Curve slider under each text block. Positive values arch the text upward, negative values curve it downward.

What formats can I download?

PNG and JPG are fully supported at multiple sizes. SVG export is available only when your chosen template was originally an SVG file. PDF and print-ready exports are marked "Coming soon".
