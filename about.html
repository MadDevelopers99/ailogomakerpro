About Logo Maker

Logo Maker is a free, browser-based tool for putting together a professional-looking logo without hiring a designer or learning design software. It's built around a simple idea: most small businesses don't need a completely original piece of art, they need a clean, recognizable mark they can put on a website, a menu, a storefront sign or a business card — quickly, and without spending money they may not have yet.

The template library covers 57 categories and more than 12,000 individual designs, organized so you can browse by industry instead of scrolling endlessly. The editor lets you change text, fonts, colors, icons, background shapes and layout, including curved text and free positioning of every element on the canvas.

This project runs entirely in your browser. There's no account system and no server storing your data — saved logos, uploads, brand kit settings and favorites are all kept locally using your browser's storage. That keeps things simple and private, though it also means clearing your browser data will remove them.

Logo Maker is an independent project and is not affiliated with design.com or any other logo design company.
