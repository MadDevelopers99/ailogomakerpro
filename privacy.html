Privacy Policy

Logo Maker is a demo project, not a commercial service, so this policy is short and literal rather than boilerplate legal text.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to a server. There's no account system, no analytics, and no tracking scripts. The "Sign In" and "Contact" forms on this site are demos and don't transmit data anywhere.

What's stored locally

Your saved logos, uploaded images, brand kit settings, favorites and homepage comments are stored using your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device — we never see it — and you can clear it any time from Settings or by clearing your browser data.

Cookies

Logo Maker itself does not set cookies. If this site displays ads (see "Advertising" below), the advertising network will set its own cookies as described there.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense and/or other third-party advertising networks. These networks use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of several third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of serving the font files, per Google's own policies. Advertising partners (see above) may also collect data per their own privacy policies. No other third-party services are used.
