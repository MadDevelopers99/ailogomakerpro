Why Customize a Casino & Gaming Logo

This is an industry built on the thrill of the experience, and a restrained, minimal logo would actively undersell that feeling before a customer even walks through the door.

Design Tips for Casino & Gaming Logos

  • Deep red, black and gold form the classic, high-recognition casino palette.
  • Card suits, dice, or a spinning wheel are instantly recognizable icons for the category.
  • Slightly ornate or decorative type suits this category better than the minimal style favored elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What colors are traditional for a casino logo?
Deep red, black and gold dominate the category and are strongly associated with luxury, risk and excitement in gambling and casino branding.
What icons work well for a casino or gaming lounge?
Playing card suits, dice, chips and a spinning wheel are the most recognized symbols and work well as a central icon in a badge-style layout.
