Why Customize a Finance & Legal Logo

Clients are handing over money or trusting legal judgment, and an overly creative logo can actually undermine confidence in a category where 'boring but solid' is the desired impression.

Design Tips for Finance & Legal Logos

  • Navy, charcoal and deep green are the safest, most trusted colors in this category.
  • A scale, column or shield icon instantly signals finance or law without needing explanation.
  • When in doubt, a clean serif wordmark alone often outperforms an icon-heavy design.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay for a law firm logo to be very plain?
Yes — in finance and legal branding, restraint reads as competence. A clean serif wordmark with minimal decoration is often the strongest choice.
What symbols are appropriate for a finance logo?
Scales, columns, shields and upward arrows are the most established symbols in the category, all reinforcing stability and growth.
