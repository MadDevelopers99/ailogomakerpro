Why Customize a Hairdressing & Barbershop Logo

Barbershops in particular lean on a strong sense of tradition and craft, and a badge-style logo with classic tool icons taps directly into that established visual language.

Design Tips for Hairdressing & Barbershop Logos

  • Scissors, combs and straight-razor icons are the clearest, most recognized symbols in the category.
  • A circular badge layout suits barbershops especially well, echoing the classic barber pole tradition.
  • Bold, slightly vintage type reinforces craft and heritage better than a modern minimal font.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a classic barbershop logo look like?
A circular badge with a scissors or razor icon and bold vintage-style lettering — a format with decades of visual tradition behind it in the trade.
Should a modern salon use the same style as a traditional barbershop?
Not necessarily — a modern salon can lean into cleaner, more minimal branding, while a traditional barbershop often benefits from the classic badge-and-tools look.
